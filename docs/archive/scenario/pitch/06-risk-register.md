# Risk Register

## Creative Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| The game feels like a procedure quiz. | Player sees correct answers, not social stealth. | Add movement pressure, sightlines, NPC observation, repair choices, and fail-forward artifacts. |
| The story feels like lore instead of play. | Pitch loses credibility. | Reject beats that cannot be observed, triggered, logged, or validated. |
| Studio location feels thematically detached. | Civic loop feels arbitrary. | Reframe Studio as public approval/records office or make its approval documents necessary for Station intake. |
| Korean tone feels generic or translated. | Core identity weakens. | Korean writer owns source text; English localizes function and coldness. |

## Technical Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Codex output tries to own game state. | Fairness and determinism break. | Proposal schema forbids state authority; backend rejects and emits fallback Evidence. |
| Codex lines are repetitive or generic. | Core AI hook fails. | Role voice cards, forbidden phrases, prompt evals, and curated fallback line bank. |
| Godot scene generation hides rules. | Players fail without understanding. | Rule-before-risk placement contract and screenshot gates. |
| Evidence becomes unreadable. | Players cannot trust outcomes. | why-line format: trigger, witness, record, delta, Station transition. |

## Production Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Team grows before core loop is proven. | Burn rises without product proof. | Core 5-6 roles only until vertical slice. |
| Vertical slice overpromises full game scope. | Funding pitch loses trust. | Slice proves one civic loop, not broad simulation. |
| Free assets look amateur. | First impression weak. | Use authored lighting, route composition, prop clusters, and text readability as art direction. |
| QA starts too late. | Narrative logic and AI regressions accumulate. | Blind comprehension tests every milestone. |

## Funding Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Pitch is too early. | Publishers see idea risk, not production proof. | Pitch collaborators first; pitch funding after AI pressure prototype or vertical slice. |
| Budget is vague. | Funding partner cannot trust planning. | Build ask from team-months, contractors, Codex worker/backend, QA, marketing, and contingency. |
| Market position says "AI" but not "game." | Audience and publishers distrust novelty. | Lead with social stealth and procedural Evidence; Codex is the reactive layer. |

## Kill Criteria

Stop or redesign the slice if:

- blind testers cannot explain why Exposure changed;
- Codex pressure does not feel better than curated barks;
- deterministic backend cannot reject invalid Codex output reliably;
- Korean text does not carry the intended institutional tone;
- the vertical slice requires more locations or systems to make the hook understandable.
