# Research Source Map

## Purpose

This map records the external research used to shape the game-design pack. It is not a bibliography for marketing copy; it is a design justification map for team decisions.

## Narrative And Systems Design

| Source | Use In This Pack |
|---|---|
| [MDA: A Formal Approach to Game Design and Game Research](https://www.cs.northwestern.edu/~hunicke/MDA.pdf) | Frames the relationship between mechanics, runtime dynamics, and intended player emotion. |
| [Failbetter: Narrative Snippets - Organising Creative Efforts](https://www.failbettergames.com/news/narrative-snippets-organising-creative-efforts-2) | Supports designing structure/state before prose and playtesting storylets early. |
| [Failbetter: StoryNexus Developer Diary #2](https://www.failbettergames.com/news/storynexus-developer-diary-2-fewer-spreadsheets-less-swearing) | Supports setup-choice-result storylet discipline and explicit quality/state changes. |
| [Game Developer: Road to the IGF - Lucas Pope's Papers, Please](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/road-to-the-igf-lucas-pope-s-i-papers-please-i-) | Supports bureaucracy as playable mechanics rather than lore decoration. |
| [Reason interview with Lucas Pope](https://reason.com/2013/09/26/papers-please-politics-in-games-and-the/) | Supports mundane procedure as a strong indie-game premise when mechanics come first. |

## Social Stealth And NPC Pressure

| Source | Use In This Pack |
|---|---|
| [Game Developer: Hitman's social cues in level design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/mapping-out-the-subtle-social-cues-throughout-i-hitman-i-s-level-design) | Provides public/public-rule/private-professional language for readable social spaces. |
| [Game Developer: The AI of Hitman (2016)](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-ai-of-hitman-2016-) | Supports predictable NPC examiners and readable behavior rather than chaotic "smart" NPCs. |
| [Game Developer: Adding Life To Worlds With Dialogue Barks](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/adding-life-to-worlds-with-dialogue-barks) | Supports barks that reinforce player action, goals, and state rather than ambient lore. |

## 3D Level And Environment Design

| Source | Use In This Pack |
|---|---|
| [The Level Design Book: Wayfinding](https://book.leveldesignbook.com/process/blockout/wayfinding) | Supports landmarks, contrast, route cues, and critical-path readability. |
