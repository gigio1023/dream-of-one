# Same Order Asset Bill Of Materials

Status: active M1 asset proof
Evidence field: `playability.assetBillOfMaterials`
Verifier: `backend/npc-runtime/src/runtime/same-order-asset-bill-of-materials.ts`

## Purpose

Same Order should meet the low-budget operation-sim quality floor with a small,
auditable Store/Station asset set. Assets clarify procedure and state; they do
not own Dream Law, suspicion, Evidence, Exposure, Station intake, verdict, or
session termination.

## Gate

The BOM passes only when:

- each local third-party pack has a local license file;
- the license is clear for commercial redistribution;
- referenced local files exist;
- Store/Station record props are visible, labelled, and generated by current
  Godot source;
- fresh visual capture and human readability review remain open until rerun.
